# ETS Code of Conduct

ETS is a technical community for evidence transparency, verification, and
claim-safe automation research.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and precise.
- Assume good faith while checking claims against evidence.
- Keep security, privacy, and claim boundaries intact.
- Use synthetic examples instead of sensitive real-world records.
- Separate technical verification from legal, medical, political, or civic
  conclusions.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing secrets, PII, restricted evidence, official election data, or legal
  records in public project areas.
- Using the project to imply unsupported claims about real-world truth, legal
  sufficiency, election correctness, vote totals, or official chain of custody.
- Posting private patent, legal, or attorney-review materials in public issues,
  discussions, or pull requests.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, close, or remove contributions that violate this
code. Serious or repeated violations may result in blocked participation.
