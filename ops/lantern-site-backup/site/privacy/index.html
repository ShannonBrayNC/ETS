PRIVACY

Privacy on the continuity site

This Azure-hosted page is a continuity copy of Lantern Protocol. It is designed to keep public product information and a contact path available if the primary site is unavailable.

Partner inquiries

The inquiry form preserves the fields and consent language used by Lantern Protocol. On this static continuity site, the form does not post the submission to Azure or a Lantern database. Instead, your browser opens your local email client with the information prepared for delivery to shannon.bray@echomedia.ai and shannonbraync@outlook.com. You decide whether to send that email.

Use of inquiry information

Information you send is used to respond to your inquiry. It is not intended to be sold or used for unrelated marketing.

Third-party media

The ETS Technical Briefing uses an ElevenLabs Audio Native player. Loading or using that player may cause your browser to communicate directly with ElevenLabs under its own privacy practices.

Continuity hosting

The continuity copy is hosted on Microsoft Azure. Standard network and service logs may be generated by infrastructure providers as part of operating their services. This static site does not add its own analytics or advertising scripts.

Contact

Questions about this continuity site or privacy can be sent to shannon.bray@echomedia.ai or shannonbraync@outlook.com.
