THE MISSING LAYER

Evidence should remain verifiable after it leaves the system that created it.

Logs, alerts, sensor readings, documents, and AI outputs move across platforms and trust boundaries. ETS preserves the evidence and the context needed to evaluate it—without replacing the systems you already operate.

QUESTIONS

Clear claims. Clear boundaries.

Does ETS replace our SIEM, XDR, data lake, backup, or case-management system?+

No. ETS is a verifiable evidence layer beneath and across existing systems. Raw evidence can remain in customer-approved storage while ETS preserves digests, provenance, relationships, checkpoints, and proof material.

Is ETS a blockchain product?+

ETS uses cryptographic commitments and append-only transparency patterns where they fit, but the architecture does not require cryptocurrency or a public blockchain. Deployment and verification profiles are policy-bound.

Can ETS operate offline?+

The edge profile is designed for capture and local verification during interruption, followed by explicit synchronization that exposes missing, conflicting, reordered, corrected, or superseded evidence.

What does patent pending cover?+

Lantern Protocol identifies ETS as patent-pending technology. Detailed claim scope is reserved for appropriate diligence and partner conversations; the site does not imply that a patent has issued.

What is available today?+

Lantern Protocol is recruiting design partners for controlled pilots, architecture assessments, and research collaborations while the product and protocol mature.
