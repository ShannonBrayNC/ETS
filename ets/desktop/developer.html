    What this candidate can—and cannot—establish

    Offline verification checks supplied material at a checkpoint. It cannot establish current log state or revocation information, real-world truth, completeness, regulatory compliance, or legal sufficiency. Reports are unsigned observations of the displayed result and policy. Production project management, remote integrations, and key custody are not implemented here. This unsigned engineering candidate is not Store-approved; clean-client, runtime, accessibility, licensing, and WACK qualification remain separate release gates.
