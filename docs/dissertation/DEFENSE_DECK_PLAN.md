## Deck Rules

- Every technical slide must include one limitation.
- Avoid decorative diagrams.
- Use figures from `FIGURE_TABLE_PLAN.md`.
- Do not show unresolved proof work as completed.
- Do not imply production deployment, legal sufficiency, or AI fairness proof.
