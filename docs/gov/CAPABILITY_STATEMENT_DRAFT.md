# ETS Capability Statement Draft

## Organization

EchoMedia.AI / Lantern Protocol

## Product

ETS - Evidence Transparency System

## Status

Patent pending. Public implementation and protocol materials only. Patent filings, USPTO receipts, claim charts, and attorney-review materials are maintained separately in a private IP repository.

## Core competency

ETS provides a tamper-evident evidence-event layer for AI-assisted and automated workflows. It validates structured EvidenceEvent metadata, canonicalizes hashable payloads, records append-only evidence events, generates Merkle-style proof material, verifies inclusion proofs, produces verification certificates, and applies policy-gated routing before evidence influences downstream automation.

## Public-sector value proposition

Government and public-sector teams need reproducible evidence trails for AI, automation, cyber, incident response, software supply chain, and compliance workflows. ETS helps establish what was submitted, what was hashed, whether proof material verifies, what certificate was generated, and whether the workflow should proceed, pause, escalate, quarantine, reject, archive, or restrict release.

## Differentiators

- Patent-pending Evidence Transparency System architecture
- EvidenceEvent lifecycle from receive, validate, canonicalize, hash, append, prove, verify, certificate, and route
- Verification certificates with explicit claim boundaries
- Policy-gated routing to automation, human review, quarantine, reject, archive, and restricted release
- Audit replay and reproducibility verification
- Cross-application verification for AI and automation workflows
- Civic and election-adjacent boundary language that avoids overclaiming regulated voting functions
- Emergency and sensor evidence ingestion model

## Primary use cases

- AI governance and auditability
- Software supply-chain evidence verification
- DevSecOps release evidence and workflow gates
- Incident, outage, and emergency telemetry evidence handling
- Inspector-general, compliance, and audit-support evidence packets
- Public-sector automation controls and human-review routing

## Claim boundaries

ETS verifies submitted-event metadata, content hashes, proof material, verification certificates, and reproducible audit records within defined protocol boundaries.

ETS does not prove real-world truth, legal sufficiency, official chain of custody, election correctness, vote totals, ballot validity, or completeness without an external expected-event policy and observation process.

ETS is not voting software, tabulation software, voter registration software, ballot software, election correctness software, or the vote of record unless separately certified and legally designated.
