# ETS Research Non-Claims

ETS is the **Evidence Transparency System**. This document states the claims the
research, alpha release, demos, and formal artifacts do not make.

## Non-Claims

ETS does not claim to prove:

- real-world truth;
- legal sufficiency;
- raw evidence authenticity;
- evidence completeness without an external expected-event policy and
  independent observation process;
- election correctness;
- vote totals, ballot validity, official results, or vote of record;
- production trust-service readiness;
- Byzantine consensus;
- Internet-scale adversarial liveness;
- hosted identity correctness;
- cloud durability;
- key custody or private-key non-compromise;
- regulatory acceptance;
- patent filing, patent allowance, or legal strategy.

## Election Demo Boundary

Election-adjacent ETS demos are evidence/audit demonstrations only. They are not
voting software, tabulation software, voter registration software,
ballot-marking software, ballot-counting software, election correctness systems,
or the vote of record.

## Formal Model Boundary

Formal models and theorem notes are scoped to their stated assumptions,
configuration bounds, fairness assumptions, and model abstractions. They do not
replace implementation tests, deployment-owner review, security review, key
management review, or operational monitoring.

## Public Language

Use:

> ETS verifies submitted-event inclusion, ordering, consistency, and reproducible
> verification for supplied proof material.

Use:

> ETS does not prove real-world truth, legal sufficiency, election correctness,
> or completeness without external policy and observation.
