# Scenario 04 — Format K

## Evidence representation

**Bounded evidence representation**

The same factual record is represented as evidence claims with explicit verification boundaries:
- E01 [evidenced fact]: Policy document version 4 was signed by the authorized agency publisher on 1 May.
- E02 [evidenced fact]: Its signature and publication digest verify.
- E03 [evidenced fact]: Version 5 superseded version 4 on 10 May.
- E04 [evidenced fact]: An external recipient retrieved version 4 from a valid archival endpoint on 14 May.
- E05 [evidenced fact]: The archive receipt proves the bytes and publisher signature for version 4.
- E06 [evidenced fact]: No evidence indicates that version 4 was still operative on 14 May.

**Boundary annotations**
- Cryptographic authenticity of version 4 is separate from current policy standing.
- The archive receipt establishes historical bytes/signature, not operativeness on 14 May.

**Strongest bounded conclusion encoded by the representation**
- Version 4 is an authentic historical publication but stale relative to version 5 and does not establish current policy standing on 14 May.

## Reconstruction questions

1. What events or states are directly supported by the evidence?
2. What conclusions are inferred rather than directly observed?
3. Which actor/action possessed valid standing at the relevant time, if established?
4. What action was requested, what action was executed, and what consequence/result was actually observed?
5. What material facts remain unknown, unavailable, contradictory, stale, or unverified?
6. What is the strongest defensible overall conclusion without exceeding the evidence?

---
Do not infer facts that are not represented. Record uncertainty explicitly when the evidence does not resolve a question.
