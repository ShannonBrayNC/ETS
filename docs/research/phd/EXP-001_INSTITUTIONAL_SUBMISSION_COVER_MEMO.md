## Study in brief

EXP-001 is a prospective within-participant comparison of three evidence-representation formats. Adult evaluators would review 12 frozen technical/operational scenarios and answer six fixed reconstruction questions. The study records free-text responses, confidence ratings, and response time, and evaluates whether representation semantics affect reconstruction errors while the underlying scenario facts are held constant.

No participant has been recruited or exposed to study materials. No participant response or outcome data exist.
