## Readiness rule

Do not mark a university route `ready for formal submission` until every mandatory admission artifact is satisfied or supported by a documented university decision. A strong repository is not a substitute for publications, supervisory fit, references, transcripts, impact, or route-specific eligibility where those are required.

The current program is **ready for supervisor engagement and doctoral-home selection, not dissertation-complete**.
