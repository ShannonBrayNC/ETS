# University-Neutral Doctoral Research Proposal

## Working title

**Bounded Evidence for Consequential Machine Actions: Formal Non-Collapse Semantics and Consequence Custody Across Distributed, AI and Cyber-Physical Systems**

Alternative continuity title:

**Independent Verification of Consequential Machine Actions: An Evidence Architecture for Distributed, AI and Cyber-Physical Systems**

## Overarching research question

**Can a distributed system produce independently verifiable evidence sufficient for an independent verifier to reconstruct and evaluate the provenance, identity, authority, policy context, decision, action and resulting state of a consequential event without requiring trust in the originating system?**

The current narrowed doctoral question is:

> **Does a formal, substrate-independent non-collapse semantics reduce unsupported evidentiary conclusions while preserving supported conclusions across distributed, AI-mediated and cyber-physical machine-action scenarios, and what additional evidence is required to support bounded consequence attribution?**

## Background and motivation

Digital systems increasingly make or execute consequential decisions. This is especially visible in AI-assisted workflows, distributed services and autonomous or cyber-physical systems. Yet post-event accountability is frequently dependent on records generated by the same origin system whose behavior is under examination.

Logs, signatures, provenance graphs, audit stores and remote-attestation mechanisms can establish valuable properties about integrity, identity, derivation, freshness or runtime state. However, those properties do not automatically establish semantic truth, correct authority, historical standing, actual execution, independently observed result or causal consequence.

The research problem is therefore no longer framed as inventing a new evidence container. It is the narrower question of whether a formal inference discipline can prevent unsupported promotion between evidentiary categories while preserving conclusions that are genuinely supported.

Examples of the relevant category boundaries include:

- integrity versus semantic truth;
- identity versus authority;
- current authority versus event-time standing;
- evidence freshness versus policy/reference freshness;
- requested action versus executed action;
- execution evidence versus resulting-state observation;
- resulting-state observation versus causal consequence;
- agreement versus independent corroboration;
- missing evidence versus evidence that an event did not occur;
- unknown, unavailable, contradicted and false states;
- source-evidence validity versus trust in the verifier.

The project investigates whether these distinctions can be given formal, machine-checkable semantics that remain stable across conventional software, AI-mediated action and cyber-physical systems.

## Relationship to existing work

The proposed research is deliberately positioned on top of established provenance, logging, attestation and assurance systems rather than against weak versions of them.

W3C PROV already provides a general model for provenance among entities, activities and agents, including rich qualified relations and provenance-of-provenance. Secure audit-log and transparency research provides tamper-evidence and historical integrity. Software supply-chain systems provide signed workflow metadata and authorized-functionary semantics. Digital forensics provides chain-of-custody methods. Remote-attestation architectures such as RFC 9334 RATS provide Attesters, Evidence, Verifiers, appraisal policy, Attestation Results, Relying Parties, freshness and explicit trust assumptions. Current RATS work further explores behavioral evidence, application-layer action/authority/outcome composition and attested AI inference receipts.

This prior art materially narrows the Evidence Architecture thesis.

An internal strongest-RATS red-team conducted before experiment execution provisionally found that all twenty frozen EXP-002 scenarios could be represented through direct RATS semantics or a reasonable rich application profile. No scenario was provisionally identified as requiring an ETS-only primitive. That internal result is not independent or confirmatory, and an external RATS/attestation challenge has been requested. It is nevertheless adverse evidence against broad novelty claims for an Evidence Object, verifier role, rich claim envelope or action/outcome receipt.

The remaining research gap is therefore more specific:

1. whether a formal cross-dimensional **non-collapse calculus** provides a measurable reduction in unsupported semantic promotion beyond an equally informative rich profile;
2. whether the same rule system can be implemented through a standards profile such as RATS+ without loss, demonstrating substrate independence;
3. whether the rules generalize across administrative, distributed, AI and cyber-physical settings; and
4. what evidentiary conditions are sufficient for **consequence custody**, where request, execution, result observation and causal/consequence attribution remain separate propositions.

The project does not claim novelty for hashes, signatures, provenance graphs, evidence envelopes, attestation, action fields, outcome fields, append-only logs or typed claims individually.

## Research objectives

The project has six primary objectives.

1. **Define formal non-collapse semantics.** Specify proposition types, epistemic states, positive inference rules, prohibited promotions, defeaters, contradiction handling and support traces.

2. **Test substrate independence.** Implement the same frozen rule set in Evidence Architecture and a strong RATS+ profile so any surviving contribution is attributable to semantics rather than proprietary packaging.

3. **Evaluate semantic-promotion error.** Prospectively test whether the calculus reduces unsupported conclusions without materially reducing supported-conclusion recall.

4. **Formalize consequence custody.** Define the conditions under which evidence may support requested action, execution, resulting-state observation and bounded consequence attribution without silently collapsing one into another.

5. **Evaluate cross-domain behavior.** Apply the same semantics across distributed software, AI/tool-mediated action and cyber-physical systems, including Ranger/VRX physical experiments.

6. **Establish failure boundaries.** Identify cases where the rules overblock, fail to generalize, duplicate existing standards practice or cannot support a conclusion from the available evidence.

## Research questions and hypotheses

The canonical programme contains RQ0 through RQ8. The narrowed hypothesis families are:

- a formal non-collapse calculus will reduce unsupported semantic promotions relative to an equally informative but unconstrained rich-profile baseline;
- the reduction will not be explained merely by refusing to issue conclusions, measured through supported-conclusion recall;
- a RATS+ implementation of the same frozen rules will behave materially equivalently to the Evidence Architecture implementation, supporting a substrate-independent rather than ETS-specific contribution;
- event-time standing cannot be established from identity or current authority alone;
- authenticated command evidence is insufficient to establish execution;
- execution evidence is insufficient to establish resulting-state observation;
- resulting-state observation is insufficient by itself to establish causal consequence;
- dependent agreeing sources should not count as independent corroboration;
- omission conclusions require an expectation or coverage model;
- some origin-side omissions remain fundamentally undetectable without independent expectations or witnesses;
- the usefulness and limits of these rules will vary by threat model and observation capability.

Each hypothesis may be supported, narrowed, revised or refuted.

## Methodology

The doctorate will combine systematic literature analysis, formal methods, generated adversarial experiments, experimental systems research and external challenge.

### 1. Literature and novelty qualification

A structured review will cover provenance models, W3C PROV, secure/tamper-evident logging, digital forensics and chain of custody, software supply-chain provenance, remote attestation and TEEs, dependable systems, assurance cases, behavioral evidence, AI audit/inference receipts, autonomous-system assurance, cyber-physical provenance and causal/consequence evidence.

Closest-work matrices will be maintained per candidate contribution. Claims that collapse into established standards/profile practice will be narrowed or retired.

### 2. Formal non-collapse semantics

The formal model will separate proposition classes such as:

- integrity;
- identity;
- provenance/source dependence;
- freshness;
- authority and event-time standing;
- requested action;
- executed action;
- resulting-state observation;
- consequence attribution;
- expectation/coverage and omission;
- verifier trust.

Rules will define both permitted derivations and forbidden unsupported promotions. Each supported derived conclusion should carry a machine-checkable support trace identifying premises, rule, event-time basis, source roots, policy assumptions and unresolved limitations.

Bounded invariants will be checked using suitable formal or property-based techniques such as Alloy, TLA+, Lean and executable property testing where appropriate.

### 3. EXP-003 — machine-checkable falsification

EXP-003 is the highest-priority prospective experiment because it directly tests the narrowed thesis without human-subject dependency.

Three conditions receive identical atomic facts:

1. a rich-profile baseline with ordinary domain claims and policy but no mandatory frozen cross-dimensional non-collapse calculus;
2. the formal non-collapse calculus;
3. a RATS+ implementation of the same rules.

A prospectively generated corpus of at least 1,000 cases, or exhaustive bounded state-space coverage, will test unsupported semantic-promotion rate, supported-conclusion recall, consequence-attribution error, historical-standing error, independence collapse and epistemic-state collapse.

A successful RATS+ implementation is expected to narrow architecture novelty while potentially strengthening the substrate-independent semantic result.

### 4. EXP-001 — evaluator reconstruction study

A separate preregistered evaluator study will test whether bounded semantics improve human reconstruction and reduce unsupported inference compared with baseline and domain-extended W3C PROV representations containing the same facts.

This study will proceed only after the appropriate institutional human-subjects/ethics determination and independent packet-equivalence review.

### 5. Distributed-system experiments

Existing asynchronous/offline ETS work will be converted into publication-grade experiments covering partition, delay, duplication, reordering, replay, omission, bounded queue pressure and later reconciliation. The research will state explicitly which continuity properties depend on fairness, healing, trust anchors or external expectations.

### 6. AI machine-action experiments

AI Witness/reference workloads will record externally inspectable input/context, model/runtime identity, policy, authorization, declared output, tool/action evidence and independent result observation where available.

The research will not require hidden chain-of-thought and will explicitly compare self-reported action/outcome evidence with independent external observation.

### 7. Cyber-physical consequence-custody experiments

Ranger R0 and VRX, or equivalent instrumented platforms, will exercise the chain:

`Observation -> Inference -> Decision -> Authority/Standing -> Requested Action -> Execution -> Result Observation -> Consequence Attribution`

Controlled failures will include sensor disagreement, stale telemetry, expired authority, command rejection, actuator non-execution, contradictory observers, delayed observation, wrong physical outcome and loss of the independent result channel.

The central measurement is not merely whether the event was logged. It is which conclusions an independent verifier is justified in making at each boundary.

### 8. External challenge and reproduction

External reviewers will be invited to defeat the claimed differentiation using strong standards-based profiles. Negative results are first-class outputs.

Where practical, experiment packages will be released for independent reproduction or counterexample construction.

## Data and evidence

The project does not initially depend on proprietary institutional datasets. Core research data can be generated prospectively from controlled systems and include:

- atomic evidence propositions and source-dependency graphs;
- generated adversarial claim combinations;
- formal proof/counterexample traces;
- Evidence Architecture and RATS+ rule-evaluation outputs;
- policy and authority state;
- AI runtime/model identity and tool-action evidence;
- cyber-physical sensor, command, actuator and resulting-state observations;
- injected-fault ground truth;
- verifier conclusions and support traces;
- performance, latency and storage measurements.

External organizational datasets may later improve ecological validity but are not required for the core thesis.

## Evaluation

Primary evaluation measures include:

- unsupported semantic-promotion rate;
- supported-conclusion recall;
- historical-standing error rate;
- command/execution/result/consequence collapse rate;
- independence-collapse rate;
- epistemic-state collapse rate;
- contradiction preservation;
- omission-boundary accuracy;
- conclusion-to-source/policy trace completeness;
- cross-domain consistency;
- formal invariant violations/counterexamples;
- computational/storage overhead;
- third-party reproduction or successful challenge.

A valid result is that a conclusion cannot be established from available evidence. The system should preserve that limitation rather than silently promote an assertion into a fact.

## Expected contribution

The strongest expected contribution is no longer a new evidence envelope.

The candidate contribution is a formally specified, experimentally falsifiable **non-collapse semantics for consequential machine-action evidence**, plus a consequence-custody specialization defining when execution and resulting-state evidence are sufficient—or insufficient—for bounded consequence attribution.

The project is intentionally structured so that a standards-based implementation can succeed. If RATS+ reproduces the same semantics, that narrows ETS-specific novelty while supporting the claim that the formal discipline is portable across evidence substrates.

The research will still be successful if some or all current ETS-specific contribution claims are refuted, provided it establishes defensible boundaries describing what can and cannot be independently supported from available evidence.

## Prospective publication plan

The revised publication sequence is:

1. **Formal non-collapse semantics for bounded machine-action evidence.**
2. **Consequence custody and independent result observation.**
3. **Distributed/offline evidence continuity under explicit assumptions.**
4. **Cross-domain evaluation of non-collapse semantics.**
5. **AI machine-action evidence with self-report versus independent observation.**
6. **Ranger/VRX cyber-physical consequence evidence and adversarial qualification.**

The Evidence Object and Evidence Graph remain important implementation artifacts but should not headline the doctoral originality claim unless later evidence justifies restoring that position.
