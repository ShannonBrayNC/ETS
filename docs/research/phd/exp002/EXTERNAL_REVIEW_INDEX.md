## Requested outcome

Please identify any case where:

- the internal RATS construction is weaker than ordinary RATS practice;
- a claimed extra rule is actually ordinary architecture/profile behavior;
- a scenario cannot preserve its bounded conclusion under the proposed RATS profile;
- relevant standards, drafts or implementations are missing;
- the comparison remains biased toward Evidence Architecture.

A conclusion that the remaining ETS distinctions collapse into an ordinary RATS application profile is a valid and useful result.
