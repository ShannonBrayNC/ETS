# Part VII — Independent Review and Human Subjects

Two major external gates remain before confirmatory evaluator exposure. They solve different problems: independent fact-equivalence review and institutional human-subjects determination. Neither has been completed.

## Why independent equivalence review matters

EXP-001 compares representations. If Condition C contains more reconstruction-material facts than A or B, the experiment confounds semantic structure with information quantity.

The independent reviewer therefore compares all three frozen packets for each scenario against the frozen fact inventory.

The reviewer checks that every material fact appears in all formats; no format adds a material fact; unknowns are not silently resolved; a local timestamp does not become trusted time; identity does not become authority; authority does not become standing; an action acknowledgment does not become execution; execution does not become consequence without evidence; shared-source dependence remains preserved; contradictions remain preserved; evidence absence does not become event absence without a coverage basis; wording does not add causality or truth; and presentation does not materially privilege one condition beyond the intended semantic manipulation.

Each scenario receives one of three permitted results: pass, fail, or pass with a documented nonmaterial difference. A failure blocks that scenario from confirmatory use until corrected prospectively.

## Why the author cannot certify independence

The author can perform internal quality control, compare packets to the fact inventory, and find mistakes. The author cannot turn that work into an independent certification merely by changing the label.

The independent-review handoff is explicit: an author-only review remains “author-only, not confirmatory ready.”

This is not a judgment about competence. It is a control against expectancy effects and self-validation.

An independent party has to perform the independent gate. The repository currently contains reviewer instructions and scaffolding. That preparation is not itself certification. No scenario is currently independently certified.

## The human-subjects issue

EXP-001 is designed as a systematic investigation intended to contribute to generalizable knowledge. It prospectively asks living adult evaluators to interact with research materials and produces analyzable response data.

The study may collect free-text answers, response time, confidence, evaluator code, and a broad experience stratum. The project should therefore conservatively treat the activity as potentially human-subjects research until a governing institution decides the applicable pathway.

The repository's human-subjects memo does not claim that EXP-001 is exempt. It does not claim that it is non-human-subject research. It does not claim expedited review. It does not claim Institutional Review Board approval.

Only the responsible institutional authority can supply the applicable determination under the chosen doctoral host or sponsor.

## Why anonymity does not automatically remove the issue

The design minimizes identity collection. That reduces privacy risk.

But de-identification alone does not necessarily remove human-subject status because interaction with participants can itself be relevant to the regulatory definition. Anonymous response data may affect the risk or exemption pathway. They do not allow the investigator to self-certify the question away.

## What has been prepared

The repository contains a submission-ready institutional review packet. That packet describes the research question, repeated-measures design, participant population, collected fields, foreseeable risks, risk-minimization approach, frozen analysis controls, and remaining gates.

It contains a cover memo requesting written guidance on the applicable review pathway. It also contains a participant-information draft.

That draft deliberately leaves unresolved details unresolved. It does not invent an approved duration. It does not promise compensation. It does not fabricate institutional contact details. It does not contain a final consent mechanism. It is marked as not approved for recruitment.

This is appropriate pre-submission work. It is not approval.

## Current recruitment state

Human evaluator recruitment is blocked.

No participant has been recruited for confirmatory data collection. No evaluator outcome data exist. No institutional determination has been obtained or claimed.

Non-human pre-execution work can continue, including artifact checks and external equivalence review. But participant exposure must wait until the governing institution has provided the required determination and any required consent, information, privacy, training, retention, recruitment, or compensation controls are satisfied.

That sequencing protects both participants and the confirmatory status of the experiment.
