## Research Profile

Enterprise architect, software engineer, cybersecurity practitioner and technical author with more than two decades of experience designing, securing and evaluating enterprise and distributed systems. Current research centres on **Evidence Architecture**: a formal and experimental investigation of whether consequential digital, AI and cyber-physical actions can produce evidence that an independent verifier can reconstruct and evaluate without requiring trust in the originating system.

The current Evidence Transparency System (ETS) research programme includes evidence-object and graph semantics, trust decomposition, distributed/offline provenance, AI machine-action evidence, cyber-physical provenance, consequence custody, formal models, reproducible experiments and adversarial qualification. Ranger R0 serves as the cyber-physical reference platform for prospective research on the chain from observation through action and resulting state.

## Current Research Programme

### Evidence Architecture / Evidence Transparency System (ETS)
**Lantern Protocol — 2026–present**

Overarching research question:

> Can a distributed system produce independently verifiable evidence sufficient for an independent verifier to reconstruct and evaluate the provenance, identity, authority, policy context, decision, action and resulting state of a consequential event without requiring trust in the originating system?

Current candidate contribution areas:

- Evidence Object model with bounded identity, integrity, provenance, custody and verification semantics;
- typed Evidence Graph relationships separating asserted from independently checkable relationships;
- explicit trust and claim-boundary decomposition;
- offline/asynchronous evidence continuity under partitions, reordering and later reconciliation;
- AI Witness / machine-action provenance without reliance on hidden reasoning traces;
- cyber-physical provenance separating observation, inference, authority, command, actuator behavior and resulting state;
- consequence custody as a distinct evidentiary problem;
- evidence-aware adversarial qualification and negative-result recording.
